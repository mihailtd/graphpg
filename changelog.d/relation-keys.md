- **Keys are detected for views, and a key column keeps its name** (#234). A simple view (one PostgreSQL can update automatically) that selects its table's key has that key, a materialized view has the key of its unique index, and any relation without a key can declare one with `@primaryKey <columns>` in its comment; `entities.<name>.primary_key` overrides them. Such views now implement `Node` and gain single-row lookups (and, when updatable, updates and deletes by key). A key column not named `id` is now a field of its own type under its own name (`patientId`) beside `databaseId`, so it is read under the name filters, conditions and `orderBy` use. **Upgrading:** the generated SDL and OpenAPI document gain these fields and operations: re-export pinned schemas.
