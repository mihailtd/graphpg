- **Extension integrations** (#169, [guide](docs/extensions.md)): graphpg detects the extensions the exposed schemas **use** (TimescaleDB hypertables, pg_jsonschema checks, `diskann` / VectorChord / PGroonga indexes, postgres_fdw foreign tables; pg_stat_statements when installed), reports them at startup and in `graphpg advise`, and lets each be switched off (`extensions.integrations.<name>: off`). The integrations themselves follow (#170–#176). Message queues (pgmq) are served through functions of your own, not directly: see the guide's recipe.
