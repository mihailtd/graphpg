- **Extension integrations** (#169, [guide](docs/extensions.md)): graphpg detects the extensions the exposed schemas **use** (TimescaleDB hypertables, pg_jsonschema checks, pgmq queues, `diskann` / VectorChord / PGroonga indexes; pg_stat_statements and pg_duckdb when installed), reports them at startup and in `graphpg advise`, and lets each be switched off (`extensions.integrations.<name>: off`). The integrations themselves follow (#170–#176).
