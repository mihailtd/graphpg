- **Event triggers** (`events:`, #47–#53, [guide](docs/events.md)): signed webhooks when rows change and on cron schedules. Changes are captured by graphpg-managed triggers in the change's own transaction (statement-level with transition tables for inserts and deletes; row-level, only on a real change, for updates), and payloads are shaped as REST rows. Delivery is at least once, claimed with leases (`SKIP LOCKED`) so any number of instances share it, woken by `NOTIFY`, retried with backoff and jitter, then dead-lettered. Requests are signed per Standard Webhooks. Cron slots are enqueued once across instances, and one-off events come from SQL (`graphpg_events.schedule()`). The webhooks are in the OpenAPI document; `graphpg events sql` prints the DDL for `install: manual`.
