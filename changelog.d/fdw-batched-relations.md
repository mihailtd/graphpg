- **Batched relations into foreign tables** (#155, `batch: true` on a virtual foreign key): a list relation reads a page of parents' children in one remote query (`= ANY` their keys, shipped by `postgres_fdw`) instead of one per parent. Measured 2.8× faster on loopback, 100 remote queries down to 1; the per-parent limit is applied locally, so it suits parents with a bounded number of children.
