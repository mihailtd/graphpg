- **Breaking: type names are English singulars** of their tables: `people` is a `Person` (`person(id:)`, was `peopleById`), `subscription_statuses` a `SubscriptionStatus` (was `SubscriptionStatuse`), `episodes_of_care` an `EpisodeOfCare` (was `EpisodesOfCare`), with irregular plurals, `-ses`/`-xes`/`-ches`/`-shes`/`-ies`, and nouns that are their own plural. Names of many rows are the type's English plural (`createPeople`, `createEpisodesOfCare`); a noun that is its own plural (`evidence`) takes its table's name (`createEvidences`) or `List`, where its bulk mutations used to be dropped.
